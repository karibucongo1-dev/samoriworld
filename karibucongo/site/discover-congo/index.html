Welcome to Karibu Congo's guide to the region. We're building it city by city, beginning with Kinshasa — where rumba, river life and a fast-changing skyline meet — and now covering Brazzaville, Pointe-Noire, Lubumbashi, Muanda, Conkouati-Douli and Garamba National Park, with more places worth the trip to follow.

Brazzaville

Across the river from Kinshasa — riverside terraces, a green-roofed basilica and gorillas a day trip away. Hotels, restaurants, day trips and flights.

Lubumbashi

The copper-belt capital of the south — lakeside hotels, the Terril slag heap, a red-brick cathedral and the Kiubo Falls. Hotels, restaurants, day trips and flights.

Available now

Kundelungu National Park

Waterfalls, savanna and camping country in Katanga — the Kiubo, Masasa, Lutshipuka and Lofoi falls, wildlife and how to get there from Lubumbashi.

Available now

Pointe-Noire

Congo’s Atlantic port city — Côte Sauvage beaches, the red cliffs of Diosso Gorge, fresh seafood and whale watching. Hotels, restaurants, day trips and flights.

Available now

Muanda

Atlantic beaches, river-mouth mangroves and nesting sea turtles on the DRC coast.

Gorillas, forest elephants, chimpanzee islands and humpback whales where the rainforest meets the sea.

Day trips from Kinshasa

Kinshasa is also a base — these are the trips worth the drive.

Available now

Zongo Falls

A 65-metre waterfall on the Inkisi River, about 130 km from Kinshasa. A long but rewarding day trip, with a riverside lodge for lunch or the night.

Available now

Lola ya Bonobo

The world's only bonobo sanctuary, just outside the city — a genuinely rare wildlife encounter.

Available now

Bombo-Lumene National Park

Savanna, forest and the fast-flowing Lumene River for hiking and swimming, about 130 km from Kinshasa.
