Kinshasa sprawls along the Congo River, facing Brazzaville across the water — the only place in the world where two capital cities look directly at each other. It's loud, fast-growing, and unmistakably itself: rumba spilling out of bars, riverside towers going up next to colonial-era streets, and a hotel scene that's modernized quickly over the last few years.

This guide covers where travelers actually stay, what's worth doing in the city, and the day trips locals recommend — Zongo Falls, Bombo-Lumene, and the bonobo sanctuary at Lola ya Bonobo among them.

Day trips

Out of the city

Kinshasa is also a base — these are the trips worth the drive.

Zongo Falls

A dramatic waterfall on the Inkisi River, roughly 3 hours from the city — a popular full-day trip.

Bombo-Lumene Reserve

Savanna and gallery forest for hiking and birdwatching, a couple of hours east of Kinshasa.

Lola ya Bonobo

The world's only bonobo sanctuary, just outside the city — a genuinely rare wildlife encounter.

Nsele River & Kingakati

Riverside villages and the Parc de la Valée de la Nsele, known locally as Kingakati.
