Lubumbashi is the capital of Haut-Katanga province and the Democratic Republic of the Congo’s second-largest city. It grew up around copper mining — founded in 1910 as Elisabethville — and still works as the country’s mining and trade hub, with the cone of the Terril slag heap on its skyline, a red-brick cathedral, lakeside hotels and a dining scene that runs from African barbecue to Lebanese-American plates.

This guide covers where travelers actually stay, where to eat, what’s worth seeing in the city — from the zoo and Lake Kipopo to the Katangese monument — and the trips further out to Kundelungu National Park and the Kiubo Falls, plus how to get here by air or by road from southern Africa.

Good to know: entry requirements

Lubumbashi is in the Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC). Visas, entry rules and border procedures change, so check the current requirements with the DRC embassy or the official immigration authority before you travel.

Things to do

In the city

A mining-town skyline, a colonial-era cathedral, a lake and a few family outings — Lubumbashi is easy to explore in a couple of days.

Le Terril de Lubumbashi

The giant cone-shaped slag heap that has become the symbol of Lubumbashi as a mining town. Built up over decades from copper-smelting waste and still reprocessed for its remaining metals, it dominates the skyline. Best seen from a distance — it sits beside an industrial site, so ask your hotel or a local guide before going closer.

Cathédrale Saints-Pierre-et-Paul

The city’s best-known landmark — red-brick Romanesque architecture from the colonial era, with a copper ceiling inside.

Monument de l’Identité Katangaise

A roundabout monument of figures holding hands above a stone arch, celebrating Katangese identity — a popular photo stop in the city.

The Garden Lubumbashi

A leisure park with a kids’ zone, pool, thatched restaurant and two aircraft on display — a good family stop.

Lubumbashi Zoo

The city’s state-run zoological garden, with zebra and antelope among the trees. Its management has reported difficulties recently and a change of partner is planned, so facilities may vary — ask your hotel before you go.

Lake Kipopo

Lakeside walks and sunsets close to the city. Novotel Lubumbashi and Pullman Grand Karavia both sit on its shores.

Day trips

Out of the city

Lubumbashi is also a base — these are the trips worth the drive. Both are long journeys, so plan an overnight stay.

Kiubo Falls

About 60 m high and 90 m wide on the Lufira River, surrounded by largely untouched forest. It is roughly five hours’ drive each way, so most visitors stay overnight at Kiubo Lodge.

Kundelungu National Park

Waterfalls and savanna on the Kundelungu plateau, including Lofoi Falls (about 340 m) and the Masasa and Lutshipuka cascades. It can be combined with a visit to the Kiubo Falls. Read the Kundelungu guide.

Getting there

Landing at Luano

Most flights arrive at Luano International Airport (FBM), about 11 km from the city center. Ethiopian Airlines connects through Addis Ababa, and flights also link Lubumbashi with Kinshasa and regional hubs. Schedules change, so compare current fares before you book.

By road from southern Africa

The main land crossing is Kasumbalesa, on the Zambian border, roughly 100 km from Lubumbashi. It is a busy freight crossing: expect queues of trucks and delays at peak times, and occasional closures.

Travel advisory: long traffic jams on the Kasumbalesa road

Long traffic jams build up on the Kasumbalesa road through Zambia because of the mineral trucks heading for the border. Allow extra time, start early, carry water and food, and check conditions before you set out.

Alternative crossing: Sakania. Sakania is another Zambia–DRC border post, reached via Ndola, and can be a quieter option when Kasumbalesa is jammed. A new post, Lonshi, opened in Sakania territory in August 2026, but it was built mainly for mining traffic and no passenger details have been published, so confirm it is open to travelers before you rely on it. Zambia and the DRC agreed in 2024 to move Kasumbalesa, Sakania, Mokambo and Kipushi to 24-hour operation — check current hours.

Get your DRC visa in advance, confirm entry rules with the embassy or official immigration authority, and cross in daylight.
