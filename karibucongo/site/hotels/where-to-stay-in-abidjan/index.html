Where to Stay in Abidjan

Abidjan wraps around a lagoon, so your base shapes how much traffic you sit in. Most visitors choose between the business-district Plateau and lagoon-side Cocody.

Plateau — Best for First Visits and Business

The Plateau is the skyline district, with the cathedral, the Salam Mosque and the Museum of Civilisations all a short ride apart. Pullman Abidjan, a polished central tower, and Noom Hotel Abidjan Plateau, a contemporary design hotel with high-floor views, are both here.

Good for: business travellers, first-timers, short stays, being near the main sights
Skip if: you want lagoon views or quiet evenings

Cocody — Best for Lagoon Views and a Calmer Base

Cocody has the lagoon views. Sofitel Abidjan Hôtel Ivoire, the city's landmark hotel, sits in lagoon-side grounds with a big pool and mid-century architecture, and is worth a visit even for a drink.

Good for: leisure stays, pool and grounds, a calmer base
Skip if: you need to cross town at rush hour, when traffic is heavy

Grand-Bassam — Best for a Beach Break

Grand-Bassam is a UNESCO-listed former colonial capital with a wide Atlantic beach, beach shacks and grilled fish. Most visitors treat it as a day trip east of the city and return before the evening traffic. Atlantic currents are strong, so swim only where locals do.

Good for: beach days, history, a slower pace
Skip if: you need to be near the city centre for meetings

Near the Airport — Best for Early or Late Flights

Félix-Houphouët-Boigny International Airport is about 16 km south-east of the Plateau, roughly 30 to 45 minutes by car depending on traffic. Leave generous time for the transfer, especially at rush hour.

Good for: early departures, late arrivals, one-night stops
Skip if: you want to see the city on foot

FAQ

How many days do I need in Abidjan?
Four or five days is enough for the Plateau, the markets and museums, a day at Grand-Bassam and a few good dinners.

When is the best time to visit?
November to March is the best time to go.

How do I get around?
Ride-hailing apps and metered taxis are the easiest way around. Traffic is heavy at rush hour, and lagoon ferries and buses are cheap but slower. Agree taxi fares before you set off.

What are the entry requirements?
Most visitors need an e-visa arranged online before travel, and proof of yellow fever vaccination is required. Rules change, so check the official government portal and your own country’s advice before booking.
