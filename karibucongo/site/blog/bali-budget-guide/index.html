Where to stay

Budget guesthouses cluster around Canggu, Ubud, and Kuta, typically running $12–18/night for a private room.

FAQ

Is $40 a day realistic for Bali?
Yes — staying in budget guesthouses, eating at local warungs, and renting a scooter keeps most travelers comfortably within $40/day outside of peak season.
What is the cheapest way to get around Bali?
Renting a scooter (around $5–7/day including fuel) is cheaper than taxis or Grab for most itineraries.
When is the cheapest time to visit Bali?
The shoulder seasons (April–June and September–October) offer lower accommodation prices with still-good weather.
Do I need to book accommodation in advance?
Not strictly, but booking 1–2 nights ahead avoids arriving late with no room, especially in Canggu and Ubud.
