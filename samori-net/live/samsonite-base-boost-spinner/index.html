Is it right for you?

Why shortlist it, and what to weigh up.

Why shortlist it

  • The capacity grows from 105 to 112.5 litres when you open the expansion zip, which helps if you buy souvenirs or pack more for the return.
  • The listing gives 3.1 kg, which leaves more of a hold weight allowance for your things than a heavier hard-shell case would.
  • A TSA lock is built into the zip closure, and the large horizontal front pocket can take a TSA padlock (not included).
  • The front pocket is shaped to hold more, and the photos show a metal logo and zip pullers on it.
  • Inside, Samsonite lists elastic cross ribbons with a buckle and a mesh side pocket in the base, plus a zipped mesh divider and extra cross ribbons in the lid.
  • Top and side handles, a bottom grip, corner protection and a kick plate are listed for the check-in sizes.

Know the trade-offs

  • It is a large check-in size, not cabin luggage, despite the word “cabin” in the Amazon title. Check your airline’s hold limits.
  • The shell is fabric, so it gives less firm protection than a hard shell. We have not confirmed how it copes with heavy rain.
  • Expanding adds 3 cm to the depth, which may take it past a stricter airline size limit, so check the expanded figure.
  • Amazon’s review summary notes that some buyers report zips breaking, and one reviewer describes a broken lock after several trips. Read recent reviews for the size and colour you choose.
  • The ratings on the listing cover every size and colour in the range, not only this one.

Who should probably look elsewhere

  • Anyone travelling cabin-only. The 55 cm sizes in the same range are the ones to look at.
  • Travellers who want a hard shell with a latch closure instead of a zip.
  • Anyone worried about zip reliability on a case that will be heavily packed and expanded.

No. At 78 × 48 × 31 cm on the listing it is a large check-in case, well beyond typical cabin allowances, even though the Amazon title uses the word “cabin”. If you want a cabin size, the same range lists 55 cm models.

That depends on the airline and fare. The listing size adds up to 157 cm across the three dimensions, 160 cm expanded, and the case weighs 3.1 kg empty. Use the checker above, then read your airline’s rules, including how it measures wheels and handles.
