Is it right for you?

Why shortlist it, and what to weigh up.

Why shortlist it

  • The shell is Curv, a self-reinforcing material that Samsonite says combines low weight with impact resistance. Amazon lists 3.1 kg for the 98 L size.
  • An integrated TSA lock is built in, so there is no padlock to carry. The two zip pulls fasten into it.
  • Smooth-running double wheels that spin 360°, a double-tube handle that is extended on check-in sizes, and a bottom grip for lifting.
  • The interior is fully lined in soft-touch fabric, with fixed dividers in both halves and removable, height-adjustable cross straps.
  • Four sizes, from the 55 cm cabin case to 81 cm, so you can build a matching set.

Know the trade-offs

  • It is a check-in size at 75 × 50 × 29 cm, so it will not go in an overhead locker.
  • Empty, it weighs 3.1 kg. With a 23 kg allowance that leaves about 19.9 kg for your packing.
  • The listing does not describe it as expandable, so you cannot add capacity.
  • It closes with a zip rather than metal latches, which some travellers prefer.
  • It sits at the premium end of hardside cases on price, and one reviewer calls it light but very expensive. The 50 ratings cover every size and colour, and the reviews shown on the UK listing are from other countries.

Who should probably look elsewhere

  • Anyone who needs a cabin bag: the Lite-Box 55 cm is the cabin size.
  • Travellers who want an expandable case, a built-in USB port or latch closures.
  • Anyone with a strict 20 kg checked-bag limit or a tight budget.

Questions

Before you pack it.

Will it fit as cabin baggage?

No. At 75 × 50 × 29 cm it is a check-in size. The Lite-Box 55 cm (55 × 40 × 20 cm) is the cabin-size model.

How much can I pack in it?

It is 98 litres and weighs 3.1 kg empty. Within a 23 kg checked-bag allowance about 19.9 kg remains for your packing; within 20 kg, about 16.9 kg.

Will it meet my airline’s size limit?

At 154 cm total it is under the 158 cm that many airlines use for standard checked bags, but wheels and handle may count towards the measurement. Use the checker above and confirm with your airline.

Does it have a lock?

Yes, an integrated TSA lock, with the two zip pulls fastening into it. The listing text names TSA007 and TSA008 in different places; the lock in the photos is marked TSA007.

Is it waterproof?

The listing does not describe it as waterproof, so we do not claim that it is.

Is it expandable?

The listing does not describe it as expandable, so you cannot add capacity.
