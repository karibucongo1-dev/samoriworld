Is it right for you?

Why shortlist it, and what to weigh up.

Why shortlist it

  • The shell is polycarbonate. Samsonite describes a self-reinforced design with extra protective corners, and the listing gives 2.9 kg for this size.
  • An integrated 3-digit TSA combination lock is built in, so there is no key to carry.
  • A double-tube handle with an integrated ID tag, and four shock-absorbing double wheels that the listing calls quiet.
  • The interior is fully lined, with a zipped divider in both the top and bottom compartment and adjustable packing straps in the bottom.
  • The range comes in four sizes, from this 55 cm case up to 81 cm, so you can match cases later.

Know the trade-offs

  • At 23 cm deep it may be over a stricter airline depth limit. Retailer listings give the 38 litre size as 55 × 40 × 20 cm, so check which size you select.
  • The listing does not describe this case as expandable, so you cannot add capacity.
  • It closes with a zip rather than a latch. Amazon’s review summary notes mixed feedback on durability, and one reviewer describes a zip failing on the larger 75 cm size.
  • One reviewer says the outside scratches fairly easily, which is worth knowing with a hard shell.
  • The ratings on the listing cover every size and colour in the range, not only this one.

Who should probably look elsewhere

  • Anyone flying with an airline that limits cabin bags to 20 cm deep.
  • Travellers who want an expandable case or a built-in USB port.
  • Anyone who wants a latch closure instead of a zip.
