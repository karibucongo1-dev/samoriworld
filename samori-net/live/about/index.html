About Samori World

Samori World is a travel guide and comparison site. We write destination and neighbourhood guides, and we link to booking partners for flights, hotels, car rental, activities and travel eSIMs.

How the site makes money

Many links and search boxes on this site are affiliate links. If you book through them, the partner may pay us a commission. It does not change the price you pay. We mark these links as sponsored, and partners do not review or approve what we write.

Who you book with

Samori World does not sell travel. When you book, your contract is with the airline, hotel, rental company or other provider shown at checkout, and they handle payment, changes, refunds and customer service. Prices and availability come from those providers and can change at any time.

How we write our guides

Guides are researched from official tourism, operator and transport sources. Unless a guide says otherwise, it is not based on a personal visit or hands-on testing. Opening hours, ticket prices and entry rules change, so check the official source linked in each guide before you travel. If you spot something out of date, please tell us.

The Samori family of sites

Samori World is the travel site in the Samori family. Our main media site is samori.io.
