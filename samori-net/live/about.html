About Samori World

Samori World is an independent travel product research site. We write guides that help you decide whether a bag or travel item suits the way you travel, before you buy.

How our guides are made

Our guides use the retailer listing, product photos and brand information. We compare size, features and who each product suits, and we note where we checked the details. Unless a page says otherwise, our guides are not hands-on reviews. Each page states this plainly, and you should confirm the exact product and current offer with the retailer before you buy.

How the site earns money

Many links on this site are affiliate links, and they are marked as sponsored. As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases, at no extra cost to you. You can read how we handle your data and cookies on our privacy page.
