Is it right for you?

Why shortlist it, and what to weigh up.

Why shortlist it

  • Padded, removable shoulder straps let you carry it like a backpack, and they can be detached when you don’t need them.
  • Reinforced haul handles, which Patagonia says are built to withstand rough baggage handling.
  • The body is 100% recycled polyester ripstop with a matte recycled TPU film laminate, described as weather- and abrasion-resistant.
  • Patagonia says the lining and webbing are also 100% recycled, and that it is made in a Fair Trade Certified factory.
  • 55 litres, which Patagonia describes as enough for a weekend or a well-organised extended trip. The listing gives 1.23 kg.

Know the trade-offs

  • At 33.8 × 24.1 × 57.9 cm on Amazon’s listing, it is larger than many airlines’ free under-seat allowances. Check yours.
  • It is a carried bag with no wheels, and a full 55 litres can get heavy over long distances. The backpack straps help, but check the fit on your own back.
  • Weather-resistant is not the same as waterproof. Patagonia’s text does not describe it as submersible.
  • The photos show a front zip pocket, but we have not confirmed the full pocket layout. Check the listing if internal organisation matters to you.
  • When we checked, the listing showed Amazon EU as the seller and said EU products may differ from UK products. Confirm the seller, shipping origin and returns arrangements.

Who should probably look elsewhere

  • Anyone with a strict under-seat allowance.
  • Travellers covering long distances on foot with a heavy load, where wheels may help.
  • Anyone who needs a hanging garment section for a suit or dress.
  • Anyone who needs a fully waterproof, submersible bag.

Questions

Before you pack it.

Will it fit as cabin baggage?

At 33.8 × 24.1 × 57.9 cm on the listing it is larger than many airlines’ free under-seat allowances. It may fit a larger cabin-bag allowance if your fare includes one, so use the checker above and read your airline’s rules, including weight.

Is it waterproof?

Patagonia describes the body fabric as weather- and abrasion-resistant. That suggests it handles rain and rough handling, but it is not the same as waterproof or submersible, and we have not confirmed how the zips and seams cope with heavy rain.

Can it be used as a backpack?

Yes. Patagonia’s description says the padded, removable shoulder straps let you carry the bag like a backpack, and that they can be detached when not in use.

Is it made from recycled materials?

Patagonia describes the body fabric, lining and webbing as 100% recycled, with a recycled TPU film laminate on the body. It also says the bag is made in a Fair Trade Certified factory.

What is the capacity?

The listing gives 55 litres. Patagonia describes that as enough for a weekend away or a well-organised extended trip. What fits depends on what you pack.

What warranty and returns should I check?
