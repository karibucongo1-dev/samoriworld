Is it right for you?

Why shortlist it, and what to weigh up.

Why shortlist it

  • A lay-flat garment section zips in hanging clothes. The photos show hanging loops, interior buckles and a fold-out extension for longer items.
  • Six interior pockets (three zipped), plus front zip pockets, side pockets and a separate shoe compartment.
  • The brand says it fits outfits for 4 to 6 days. The shoulder strap is described as removable and adjustable, and a luggage sleeve slides over a suitcase handle.
  • A matching quilted toiletry bag with an interior zip pocket comes with it.

Know the trade-offs

  • The brand says it is too large to count as a personal item. The body measures about 51 × 30 × 33 cm (20 × 12 × 13 in), so check that against your airline’s allowance.
  • Check the zips, seams and strap attachments on arrival, before you need the bag for a trip.
  • It is a carried bag with no wheels, and a full duffel this size can get heavy over long distances.
  • Confirm the current shipping origin and returns arrangements; an imported offer may differ from a local one.

Who should probably look elsewhere

  • Anyone with a strict under-seat allowance.
  • Travellers covering long distances on foot with a heavy load, where wheels may help.
  • Anyone carrying very long coats or dresses, unless the roughly 97 cm (38 in) garment length shown in the brand’s diagram is long enough.
  • Anyone who needs a guarantee of crease-free arrival.
