Privacy and cookies

Who we are

Samori World (samori.net) is responsible for the personal data described on this page. Contact: info@samori.net.

What this site does

Samori World compares flights, hotels, car rental, activities and eSIMs and links to partners who pay us a commission if you book. We do not have user accounts and we do not sell anything ourselves.

Cookies and similar technologies

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to allow the optional items below. Your choice is kept in your browser for 12 months. You can change it at any time with “Cookie settings” in the footer.

Affiliate links

Many links on this site are affiliate links (Travelpayouts partners such as KKday, Klook and Aviasales, Amazon Associates and Impact). If you buy through them we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. They are marked sponsored.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data we hold about you, object to its use, or withdraw consent at any time. Email info@samori.net. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
