Questions

Before you pack it.

Which size should I choose?

Medium is enough for a weekend kit or travel-size bottles. Choose large if you carry full-size shampoo and conditioner, or share the bag with someone else.

Can I take the clear pouch through airport security?

The clear zip pouch is designed to hold travel-size liquids so you can lift them out at screening. Liquid rules differ by airport and country, so check your airline's current limits before you fly.

Is it waterproof?

The outer material is water-resistant, so splashes and damp counters wipe off. It isn't designed to be submerged.

What can it hang from?

The swivel hook fits most door tops, towel rails, shower hooks and hanging rods. It also stands flat on a counter or shelf when there's nowhere to hang it.
