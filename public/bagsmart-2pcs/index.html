The 3-1-1 rule, simplified

If it's 100 ml or less, it goes in.

Bottles of 100 ml or under go in the clear bag; anything bigger belongs in your checked luggage. Keep the bag packed with your travel-size favourites and you'll never have to sort through toiletries at the airport again.

Is it waterproof?

The bag is water-resistant, so a leaking bottle stays inside and splashes wipe off. It isn't designed to be submerged.
